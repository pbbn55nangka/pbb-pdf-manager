Dasar Privasi / Privacy Policy

Berkuat kuasa / Effective: 21 Oktober 2026

1. Siapa kami / Who we are

PBB PDF Manager ialah alat dalaman yang dikendalikan oleh Cawangan PBB N55 Nangka untuk memproses borang permohonan keahlian PBB N55 Nangka.

PBB PDF Manager is an internal tool operated by Cawangan PBB N55 Nangka to process PBB N55 Nangka membership application forms.

2. Data yang diakses / Data we access

  • Gmail: hanya e-mel notifikasi borang daripada Jotform dan lampiran PDF borang tersebut. / Only Jotform form-notification e-mails and their PDF attachments.
  • Google Drive: folder borang keahlian milik pengendali, untuk menyimpan dan menyusun PDF borang. / The operator's membership-form folders, to store and organise the form PDFs.
  • Google Sheets: senarai rekod permohonan milik pengendali. / The operator's application tracking sheet.

Maklumat dalam borang termasuk nama, nombor kad pengenalan, alamat, nombor telefon dan maklumat keahlian pemohon. / Form contents include applicants' names, identity card numbers, addresses, phone numbers and membership details.

3. Tujuan / Purpose

Data digunakan semata-mata untuk memproses, mengesahkan, mencetak dan menghantar borang permohonan keahlian kepada ibu pejabat parti. Data tidak dijual, tidak digunakan untuk pengiklanan, dan tidak dikongsi dengan pihak lain selain untuk tujuan tersebut.

Data is used solely to process, verify, print and submit membership applications to the party headquarters. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else except for that purpose.

4. Penyimpanan dan keselamatan / Storage and security

Data disimpan dalam akaun Google pengendali (Gmail, Drive, Sheets) dan dalam projek Google Cloud pengendali (rantau Singapura). Akses terhad kepada pengendali yang dibenarkan sahaja. Kelayakan akses disimpan dalam Google Secret Manager.

Data is stored in the operator's Google account (Gmail, Drive, Sheets) and in the operator's Google Cloud project (Singapore region). Access is limited to authorised operators. Access credentials are kept in Google Secret Manager.

5. Data Pengguna Google / Google user data

PBB PDF Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Tempoh simpanan / Retention

Rekod disimpan selama diperlukan untuk urusan keahlian dan rekod parti, kemudian dipadam mengikut keperluan. / Records are kept as long as needed for membership administration and party records, and deleted when no longer required.

7. Hak anda / Your rights

Pemohon boleh meminta akses, pembetulan atau pemadaman data mereka dengan menghubungi kami. / Applicants may request access to, correction of or deletion of their data by contacting us.
